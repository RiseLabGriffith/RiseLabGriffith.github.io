## R — Responsible and Resilient Cybersecurity

We develop principles, methods and evidence for systems operating in complex and adversarial environments.

- Robustness, safety and resilience
- Transparency and accountability
- Evaluation, verification and continuous assurance

## I — Intelligent Cyber Defence and Security Analytics

We develop intelligent, data-driven and automated methods for understanding threats and supporting effective cyber defence.

- Threat intelligence and security analytics
- LLM, foundation-model and agent security
- Secure software engineering and testing

## S — Secure and Privacy-Preserving Foundations

We develop mathematical, cryptographic and systems foundations for protecting sensitive data, computation and digital interactions.

- Privacy-preserving data processing
- Secure computation and collaborative analytics
- Cryptographic protocols for intelligent systems
- Verifiable and accountable computation

## E — Engineering, Evaluation and Continuous Operations

We translate principles, defensive capabilities and technical foundations into deployable cybersecurity outcomes.

- Cross-layer demonstrators
- Engineering methods and toolchains
- Evaluation and red-team pipelines
- Operational monitoring and assurance
