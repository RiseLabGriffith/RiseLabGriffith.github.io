## Prospective HDR Students

We welcome enquiries from prospective Higher Degree by Research students interested in cybersecurity, trustworthy AI, cryptography, privacy or secure software engineering. Scholarship and project opportunities are subject to availability and eligibility.

## Visiting Researchers

Researchers and students interested in a research visit may contact relevant RISE academic members to discuss alignment and available capacity.

## Academic Collaboration

RISE welcomes collaboration that combines complementary expertise and contributes to secure, private and resilient digital or intelligent systems.

## Industry and Government Partnerships

We welcome conversations about collaborative research, security evaluation, research translation and project-supported demonstrators.
